# Working style

Work through multi-step tasks **one step at a time**. Always:

1. Give a short numbered map of all the steps first, so the whole path is visible.
2. Then present **only the current step** in detail: what we are doing, why it
   matters, and the exact commands or clicks.
3. **Stop and wait** for confirmation that the step worked before moving on.
   Do not bundle several steps into one message.
4. When the step is confirmed, say what changed, then move to the next step.

If a step fails, diagnose that step before continuing — never proceed past a
failure or work around it silently.
